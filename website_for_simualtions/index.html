About the author

Physicist by training. Quant by profession. Teacher by choice.

  • EducationBS-MS, Indian Institute of Science
  • CertificationNISM Research Analyst
  • CertificationPMS Certified
  • Based inIndia

I studied at the Indian Institute of Science on its five year BS-MS programme, where two interests took hold at the same time: the structure of physics, and the pleasure of turning an idea into something you can actually operate.

Professionally I work with markets. I am a NISM certified Research Analyst and PMS certified, and I build algorithms for financial markets and data: research pipelines, systematic strategies and risk models. The habit is the same one physics teaches you, describe the system honestly, then test the description against reality.

The rest of my time goes into teaching. Most students do not struggle with physics itself, they struggle with physics that only ever exists on paper. So I build these simulations: small worlds where you can pull a string, tilt a wedge or flip a switch and watch the equations respond. A concept you can feel does not have to be memorised.
